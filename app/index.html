One trophy can pass you by. Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) has a first meditation at Jason’s house that only exists in Chapter 1 — after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept. Keep a manual save from Chapter 1. Everything else can be done after the story.

Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no online trophies, about 200 hours by the guides’ estimates, difficulty around 4/10. The story takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist is the long tail. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them until every trophy is earned. There is no mission replay — keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter.

    Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, cross-checked on Exophase and PSNProfiles. Every guide is written for this page, in its own words, and cites its sources beside each trophy.
